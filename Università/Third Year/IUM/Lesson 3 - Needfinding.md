One of the first phases of development is the __needfinding__.

A need is something that is __lacking__ in a system, a gap. So by needfinding one can discover opportunities by recognizing these gaps.

Often this means finding potential __user needs__. Also required are:
- Information about the users.
- How are they doing the lacking activity meanwhile. (The need)
- In what context are they doing it.

# Needfinding Methods

There are many techniques adopted in needfinding and requirement analysis:
- Observation, like an ethnographic research.
- Diaries.
- Interviews.
- Focus groups.
- Surveys.
